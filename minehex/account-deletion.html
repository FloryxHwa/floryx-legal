MineHex — Account & Data Deletion

App: MineHex (Android)
Developer: Floryx
Last updated: 16 May 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data for the mobile game MineHex, published by Floryx.

1. What account does MineHex use?

MineHex does not require you to create an account. The game can be played fully anonymously.

If you choose to use cloud save and achievements, MineHex uses Google Play Games Services (your Google account on the device). We do not hold a separate username, password, or email address for you.

2. How to request deletion

Send an email to contact.floryx@gmail.com with the subject line MineHex Account Deletion Request.

In the email, include:

  • Your Google Play Games Services display name (the nickname you see in MineHex when signed in), or
  • Your Play Games Services player ID (visible in the Google Play Games app under your profile), or
  • The Google account email address you used to sign in to MineHex.

We process deletion requests within 30 days and will reply to your email to confirm completion.

3. What gets deleted

Data Action on request
Cloud save (level progress, settings) Deleted from Google Play Games Services within 30 days
Achievements unlocked in MineHex Reset / removed from your Play Games profile within 30 days
Any record we hold linking your player ID to MineHex Deleted within 30 days

4. What is NOT deleted (and why)

Data Reason How to delete
Local game data on your device Stored only on your phone, not on any Floryx server Uninstall the App, or clear app data in Android Settings
Your overall Google account Owned by Google, not Floryx Manage at myaccount.google.com
Anonymized AdMob data (ad impressions, frequency capping) Not personally identifiable; held by Google, not Floryx Reset your Android Advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads
Aggregated, non-identifiable analytics Cannot be linked back to an individual user (no action needed)

5. Retention period for residual data

Once your deletion request is processed, the only data Floryx may retain is a record of the deletion request itself (your email and the date), kept for up to 2 years. This is required to demonstrate compliance with data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA) if requested by a regulator. This record is not used for any other purpose.

6. Questions

For any questions about this deletion process or your data rights under GDPR, CCPA, or other privacy laws, contact: contact.floryx@gmail.com.

See also the full MineHex Privacy Policy.
