This Privacy Policy describes how Floryx ("we", "us") handles information when you use the mobile game MineHex ("the App") on Android devices.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

We do not require account creation. The App does not ask for your name, email, or other personal identifiers.

Information collected automatically

  • Device identifiers — Android Advertising ID, used by advertising and analytics SDKs listed below.
  • Device & technical info — device model, OS version, language, crash logs, performance metrics.
  • Gameplay data — level progress and settings. Stored locally on your device.

Purchases

The App is free and contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions. It is supported by advertising only.

2. Third-Party SDKs

The App integrates the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unreal Engine (game runtime, optional analytics) — epicgames.com/site/privacypolicy

3. How We Use Information

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the App
  • Display advertising (including personalized ads where permitted)
  • Diagnose crashes and technical issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Children's Privacy

MineHex is rated for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EEA, UK, or California, you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Withdraw consent for personalized advertising (in-app settings)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, email contact.floryx@gmail.com.

6. Data Retention

Local gameplay data persists on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data.

7. Data Transfers

Third-party SDKs may transfer data outside your country of residence (e.g., to the United States). These providers contract to use appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the App's update notes. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version.
